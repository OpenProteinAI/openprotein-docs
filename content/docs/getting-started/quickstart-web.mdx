Step 1: Request [early access](https://openprotein-ai.webflow.io/early-access-form)

Step 2: [Log in](https://app.openprotein.ai) to your account

Step 3: Get started using our [web application](/web-app) for your protein engineering goals

<Callout title="Are you a developer?">
  Visit [Get started with our API](/getting-started/quickstart-api).
</Callout>

## Quick start tips

Once you log in, you'll see your home view and can navigate to get started with our tools.

![The OpenProtein.AI home view](/_static/getting-started/tips.png)

Do you want to...

<TipRow icon="/_static/getting-started/poet-icon.png" question="Make sequence predictions or designs without using any data?">
  Get started with [PoET](/web-app/poet), which uses evolutionary information to generate
  protein sequences.
</TipRow>

<TipRow icon="/_static/getting-started/bar-chart.png" question="Analyze your experimental data for library design?">
  Create your first project and deploy machine learning models trained on your data.
  [Learn more about OP Models](/web-app/opmodels) or
  [get started uploading your data](/web-app/opmodels/uploading-your-data).
</TipRow>

<TipRow icon="/_static/getting-started/dna-broken.png" question="Explore your protein's structure?">
  Use our [Structure Prediction tool](/web-app/structure-prediction/using-structure-prediction)
  to view and download high quality images.
</TipRow>
