## Logging into your account

Log in to the [web platform](https://app.openprotein.ai) with your username and password.

![The OpenProtein.AI login screen](/_static/getting-started/login.png)

You can also log in from our [home page](https://openprotein.ai).

![Logging in from the OpenProtein.AI home page](/_static/getting-started/acct-login-homepage.png)

<Callout title="Don't have access yet?">
  Request [early access](https://openprotein-ai.webflow.io/early-access-form).
</Callout>

## Resetting your password

To change your password, navigate to the account icon on the top right corner of the page,
and select **Change password**.

![Changing your password from the account menu](/_static/getting-started/change-password.png)
