**Tutorials** focus on individual tools and operations, often through a practical example. They are grouped into three broad functions: *Prepare & Generate*, *Model & Predict*, and *Analyze*. These are not fixed stages—start wherever makes sense for your project.

For deeper background on the ideas behind the tools, see our **[Concepts](/concepts)** docs.
