**Using these guides**

- Start from the task at hand, not from the top of the list. Each guide stands on its own.
- Check the **Before starting** section at the top of each guide. It lists the required inputs and links to the guides that produce them. For example, training a model requires an uploaded assay dataset.
- Follow the steps in order. Each guide ends with **Next steps** pointing to related tasks.

Guides are grouped by goal: *Prepare & Generate*, and *Model & Predict*. Most projects draw on guides from more than one group.

For step-by-step learning, see the **[Tutorials](/tutorials)**. For background on the ideas behind the tools, see **[Concepts](/concepts)**.
